Our Story

Founded in Vadodara, Serving the World

INDUSTRIO NEGECIO was established in 2011 in Vadodara, Gujarat, at the heart of India's petrochemical belt. What began as a regional sourcing desk for refinery spares has grown into a full-service supply, procurement outsourcing and management services company for the global oil & gas industry.

Today we support upstream drilling contractors, midstream pipeline operators and downstream refiners with equipment sourcing, vendor management and technical manpower, backed by a quality-first approach to every order we fulfil.

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
  • API-compliant sourcing network
  • Pan-India warehousing with global freight partners
