Our Story

Founded in Vadodara, Serving the World

INDUSTRIO NEGECIO was founded in 2024 in Vadodara, Gujarat, at the heart of India's petrochemical belt, by a team with decades of combined experience in industrial procurement. From day one, we set out to run as a full-service supply, procurement outsourcing and management services company for the global oil & gas industry, not a regional sourcing desk that grows into one over time.

In our first two years we've supported upstream drilling contractors, midstream pipeline operators and downstream refiners with equipment sourcing, vendor management and technical manpower, backed by a quality-first approach to every order we fulfil.

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
  • API-compliant sourcing network
  • Pan-India warehousing with global freight partners
